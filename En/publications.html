Post-processed frozen-flow methods for the long-time sampling of ergodic dynamics on Riemannian manifolds
Adrien Busnot Laurent, Sébastien Macé
PDF | HAL | arXiv

Abstract: This paper proposes frozen-flow methods with and without post-processors for the long-time sampling of ergodic dynamics on Riemannian manifolds, with applications to the simulation of the Riemannian Langevin equation.
